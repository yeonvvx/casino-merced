Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Casino Merced collects through casinomerced.com, how it's used, and the choices you have. It applies to this website only, not to anything collected in person at the casino.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into one of our two forms:

  • Prize list signup: your email address and your confirmation that you are 21 or older and agree to receive promotional emails.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, an optional subject and your message, so we can reply.

We don't ask for your phone number, payment details, date of birth or location, and the site has no accounts, analytics or advertising tools.

How we use it

We use your signup email only to send news about promotions, prizes and events at Casino Merced. We use contact form details only to answer your message.

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent or share your information for advertising. Your email is stored with our email service provider, [provider name, e.g. Mailchimp], which sends our emails on our behalf and may not use your address for anything else. When you send the contact form, it opens your own email app with your message addressed to us, so it reaches us as a normal email. We may disclose information if required by law.

Other services this site loads

Some photos on this site are loaded directly from Wikimedia Commons, so your browser connects to Wikimedia's servers to show them, which lets Wikimedia see your IP address. See the Wikimedia privacy policy. Our fonts are stored on this site, not loaded from Google or any other font service. Links to Google Maps, PokerAtlas and Instagram take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies and does not use tracking, analytics or advertising tools, so there is no cookie banner. If you change a setting in the accessibility menu (such as text size or contrast), your browser remembers it in its own local storage so the setting stays on the next page. That choice never leaves your device and is not sent to us; resetting the menu or clearing your browser data removes it. If we ever add cookies or tracking, we'll update this section and ask for your consent first.

Do Not Track

Because this site doesn't track you, it behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We don't track you across other websites.

Unsubscribing, access and deletion

Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and one click removes you from the list. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, call (209) 233-9369 or email khonglai@casinomerced.com. We'll confirm the request and delete your information within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

How long we keep it

We keep your signup email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep contact form messages only as long as needed to answer them, and no longer than 12 months.

Age

This site and our promotions are for adults 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 21. If you believe someone under 21 has given us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
