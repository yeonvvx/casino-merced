Blackjack

Two versions

Pure 21.5 Buster and Pure 21.5 Spanish Blackjack.

  • Buster: get as close to 21.5 as you can without going over. An ace with a ten or face card as your first two cards is the best hand and pays 6 to 5.
  • Spanish: the tens are out of the deck. Split up to three times, double down more than once, and 21 wins automatically.
