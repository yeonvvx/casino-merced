Signs it may be time for a break

  • Playing longer or with more money than you planned.
  • Chasing losses, or borrowing to play.
  • Hiding how much you play from people close to you.
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when you aren't playing.

Tips for keeping it fun

  • Set a budget and a time limit before you sit down, and stick to them.
  • Treat money spent at the table as the cost of a night out.
  • Take breaks, and don't play to escape stress or win back losses.
